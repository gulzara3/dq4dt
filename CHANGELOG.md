# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-06)

First public release, matching the manuscript submitted to IEEE Transactions on Reliability.

- Eight degradation operators with severity-to-quality mapping.
- Three surrogate architectures with heteroscedastic heads and post-hoc sigma calibration.
- Resumable sweep over two C-MAPSS subsets, five seeds, and six severity levels (1,440 cells).
- Elasticity, breakpoint, cross-dataset, interaction, signature, injection-point, and gating analyses.
- Live data-quality monitor.
- Released results and figures from the paper run.
- 41 CPU-only unit tests.
